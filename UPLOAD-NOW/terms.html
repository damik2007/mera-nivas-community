3.What the platform provides

Mera Nivas brings the everyday running of a society into one place, replacing scattered chat groups, paper notices and the gate register:

  • Billing — maintenance cycles, one-off charges such as fines or damage, balances, online payment and downloadable receipts.
  • Notices — society announcements sent to everyone, a street, a road or chosen residents, with optional PDF/JPG/PNG attachments.
  • Events — resident- or admin-created events, selective or society-wide invitations and RSVPs.
  • Guest management — time-bound QR passes, shareable pass links, gate check-in and check-out and a visitor log.
  • Parking — guest slot allotment on check-in and release on check-out.
  • Directory and admin tools — streets, roads, houses, residents, family members and optional legal documents.

Features can be added, changed or removed over time. Some features depend on third-party services (see “Third-party services”) and may be switched off if those are not configured.

5.Your responsibilities

  • Keep your password and any reset link confidential. Do not share your login with neighbours, staff or guests.
  • Give accurate information about yourself, your household members and your guests, and keep it up to date.
  • Approve guests only for visits you genuinely expect, and revoke passes you no longer need.
  • Treat other residents’ details in the directory as private. Do not copy, scrape, sell or use them for marketing or any purpose outside community life.
  • Pay bills you owe by their due date, and raise disputes with the admin promptly.

6.Guest passes and gate entry

A guest pass is a signed, time-bound QR token tied to the resident who approved it. It works only inside its visit window, and a single-use pass stops working after one visit. Multi-use passes can be checked in again on later visits within their window.

  • The approving resident is responsible for the guest they invite and for the guest’s conduct inside the society.
  • Scanning a pass inward records a check-in and may allot a parking slot. Scanning the same pass outward records a check-out and frees the slot.
  • Security staff may refuse or end entry at any time, for example for safety, an invalid pass or a society rule. A reason is recorded.
  • Anyone holding a pass link can open that pass’s QR code and status without logging in. Share it only with the guest it was made for. If it is forwarded further, revoke the pass.

A pass is a convenience for gate staff, not a guarantee of entry and not proof of a guest’s identity.

7.Parking

Guest parking slots are allotted automatically at check-in and released at check-out. A slot can be linked to one active visit at a time. Availability is not guaranteed, and the admin may block, reassign or change slots. Vehicles are parked at the owner’s risk; Mera Nivas does not provide security, insurance or custody of vehicles.

8.Billing and payments

Bills can be regular maintenance cycles, shown with a category breakdown, or one-off charges such as fines or repairs, shown with the admin’s stated reason. Each bill shows the amount paid and the balance due.

  • Online payments are processed by Razorpay when enabled. A bill is marked paid only after the server independently verifies the payment signature; the app never trusts a payment result reported by your device alone.
  • Each payment ID can be credited once. If money is debited but a bill stays unpaid, keep the payment reference and contact the admin.
  • Receipts are generated for successful payments and can be viewed or saved as PDF by the paying resident and the society’s admins.
  • Where Razorpay is not configured, a built-in test flow may appear. It does not move real money and does not settle real dues.
  • Amounts, due dates, fines and late-fee rules are set by the society, not by KushalLabs. Mera Nivas records them but is not a party to the society’s financial arrangements.
  • Refunds and chargebacks are handled between you, the society and the payment provider under the provider’s rules.
