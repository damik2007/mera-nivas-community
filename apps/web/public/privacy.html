1.Overview

This policy explains how Mera Nivas, built and operated by Magam Kushal Reddy (KushalLabs), handles personal data under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law.

  • We collect only what a society needs to run billing, notices, events, visitors and parking.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.
  • Your society admin decides which residents and records exist; KushalLabs provides and maintains the software and hosting.

2.Who is responsible

For resident records the society, through its admin, decides what is collected and why. KushalLabs operates the platform on the society’s behalf and handles the data only to provide the service. For privacy questions or requests, contact your admin or us at kushalreddymagam@gmail.com.

4.How we use it

  • To create and secure accounts and show each person only what their role allows.
  • To raise bills, take and verify payments and issue receipts.
  • To deliver notices, event invitations, reminders and in-app notifications to the intended audience.
  • To issue and validate guest passes, record entries and exits and allot parking.
  • To send password-reset links and other service emails.
  • To detect abuse, investigate failed sign-ins, keep an audit trail and fix faults.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use personal data for profiling, advertising or unrelated marketing.

5.Why we may process it

We rely on your consent, given when you sign in and use the platform, and on the legitimate uses the law recognises, such as providing a service you have asked for, complying with the law and keeping the service secure. You can withdraw consent by asking to close your account, although some records may have to be kept (see “Retention”).

7.Cookies and local storage

Mera Nivas does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It stores two items in your browser’s local storage, an access token and a refresh token, so you remain signed in. They are removed when you sign out. Google’s sign-in button and Razorpay’s checkout may set their own cookies under their own policies. Clearing your browser data signs you out.

8.How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • Traffic uses HTTPS. Sessions use short-lived access tokens with refresh tokens, and a password reset invalidates earlier sessions and reset links.
  • Password-reset links expire after one hour, and the reset flow does not reveal whether an email has an account.
  • Sign-in, password-reset and guest-pass endpoints are rate limited to slow guessing attacks.
  • Access is checked on the server by role and ownership. For example, a receipt can be opened only by its owner or an admin of the same society.
  • Database queries are parameterised, uploaded documents are stored outside the public web folder and are served only through authenticated download, and guest QR tokens are signed.
  • Payments are confirmed server-side by verifying Razorpay’s signature.

No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password, sign out on shared devices, and tell your admin quickly if something looks wrong.

9.How long we keep it

We keep data while your account is active and as long as the society needs it for its records. Financial records such as bills, payments and receipts, and security and audit logs may be kept longer for accounting, dispute and legal reasons even after an account is closed. Personal details that are no longer needed are deleted or anonymised.

10.Your rights and choices

  • Access the personal data held about you and get a summary of how it is used.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Erase data that is no longer needed, subject to records we must keep.
  • Withdraw consent or close your account by asking your admin.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot.
  • Complain to us first, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

To use a right, email kushalreddymagam@gmail.com from your registered address, or ask your society admin. We will verify who you are and respond within a reasonable time.

11.Guests and children

Residents who enter guest details should only do so with the guest’s knowledge. Guests may ask the host or the admin to correct or remove their details. Mera Nivas is not directed at children; children’s records are added only by an adult resident or the admin as part of a household, and are used only for society administration.

12.Where data is processed

Data is stored on hosting servers that may be located outside India. Google and Razorpay may process data in other countries under their own safeguards. We use such providers only to deliver the service.

13.If something goes wrong

If a personal-data breach affects you, we will tell the society admin and, where the law requires, the affected people and the Data Protection Board, with what happened, what data was involved and what to do next.

14.Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or laws change. The “last updated” date shows the current version, and significant changes will be announced in the app or via your admin.
